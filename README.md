# MailSenpai

Email marketing platform and SMTP relay, made in Italy and hosted in the EU.

- 🌐 **Website:** https://www.mailsenpai.com · English: https://en.mailsenpai.com
- ✉️ **SMTP Senpai**, managed SMTP relay for apps, websites and SaaS: https://en.mailsenpai.com/smtp-senpai/
- 🤖 **Remote MCP server**: connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI assistants to your MailSenpai account. Endpoint `https://mcp.mailsenpai.com/mcp` (OAuth 2.1) · [mailsenpai/mailsenpai-mcp](https://github.com/mailsenpai/mailsenpai-mcp)

## What you can do

- Newsletters, automations, segments and statistics in one place
- App and transactional email through SMTP Senpai, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up for your domain
- Manage lists, subscribers, templates and campaigns by asking your AI assistant
